# Full-Rebuild Workflow

Status: `IN_PROGRESS`. Authority: `FULL_REBUILD_CONTRACT.md` → `audit-musk.md` → this workflow. `WORKFLOW.md` remains the historical gap-remediation record and cannot narrow this scope.

## Gate 0 — Authority reset

Freeze benchmark and learner-history protections; authorize replacement of curriculum/application learning surfaces; register all 118 capabilities and the 30-day acceptance contract.

Exit: rebuild contract, authority map, current status and failing-first validator agree.

## Gate 1 — Thirty-day architecture

Design one coherent progression across Days 1–30: prerequisites, learning tree, workload, reviews, retrieval spacing, support fading, build outputs and sealed final. Assign all M1–M100/P1–P18 individually without exposing benchmark prompts.

Exit: every ID has a unique semantic route and every day has a feasible purpose; mapping alone is not content PASS.

## Gate 2 — Daily rebuild batches

Rewrite authoritative day sections in bounded batches: Days 1–5, 6–10, 11–14, 15–19, 20–24, 25–29, then Day 30/whole-course closure. For every batch update lesson, plan, evaluators, rubrics, coverage, payload and HTML source together.

Exit per batch: all daily functions render from the canonical source, semantic review passes every assigned capability, workload is explicit and no detached legacy/remediation appendix remains.

## Gate 3 — Full semantic ledger

Review all 118 rows for mechanism correctness, prerequisites, assumptions, complete worked reasoning, independent practice, materially changed transfer, advanced boundary, evaluator, feedback, changed retry and delayed retrieval.

Exit: 118/118 curriculum-readiness decisions PASS; no blanket evidence.

## Gate 4 — Progression and workload

Verify weekly retrieval, fading support, increasing ambiguity/difficulty, dependency order, recovery feasibility and build-test-feedback-iterate artifacts. Pilot workload where evidence is available.

Exit: no mandatory work hidden in stretch; uncertain workload remains PENDING rather than assumed feasible.

## Gate 5 — Application delivery

Verify Markdown→payload byte equality and that HTML renders the authoritative day contract, correct evaluators, evidence states and key gates. Browser behavior and visual quality remain distinct checks.

Exit: static delivery PASS plus browser PASS; user may defer visual preference, but not content visibility/functionality.

## Gate 6 — Final-output system

Verify baseline comparability, weekly evidence ledger, sealed parallel final protocol, five domain floors, all-capability ledger and ability to test similar/difficult/advanced unseen work.

Exit: curriculum can produce credible evidence; it does not claim the learner has produced it.

## Gate 7 — Independent re-audit

Re-audit all 30 days, 118 capability routes, application delivery, anti-memorization and final-output acceptance against fresh evidence. Preserve the original `AUDIT_REPORT.md` as historical snapshot and produce a new report.

Exit: curriculum readiness, product delivery and learner mastery are reported separately with no inherited PASS.

## Batch handoff

Record days, IDs, files, source/payload result, rendered-section result, semantic decision, transfer adjudication, workload status, learner-evidence status, remaining risks and next batch. A failed or pending mandatory gate cannot be renamed PASS.
