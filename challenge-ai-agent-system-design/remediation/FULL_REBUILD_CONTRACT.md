# Full 30-Day Curriculum Rebuild Contract

Status: `AUTHORITATIVE`. This contract supersedes gap-only remediation whenever the two conflict.

## Product outcome

The deliverable is one coherent 30-day learning system, not an audit-gap patch set. After the challenge, a successful learner must be able to reconstruct solutions for all 100 benchmark capabilities, solve materially different unseen variants, handle difficult/advanced cases, and design and defend AI-agent systems from first principles. Completion of 30 sessions alone is not success; evidence and a sealed final decide the result.

## Complete rebuild scope

1. Rewrite the authoritative learning surface for every Day 1–30. Existing text may be reused only after semantic review; unchanged legacy text is not presumed acceptable.
2. Cover and individually trace all `M1–M100` and `P1–P18`. The 100 questions and PDF are capability benchmarks, never daily lesson banks.
3. Organize each day as a coherent learning journey. IDs live in the evidence ledger and evaluator routing; they must not turn the lesson into a disconnected checklist.
4. Update `PLAN.md`, `LESSONS.md`, public assessments, protected rubrics, `COVERAGE_MAP.md`, generated payload and HTML delivery together in the same approved daily batch.
5. Do not defer content-to-HTML synchronization to a later phase. Browser interaction/visual approval may be later, but source/payload/rendered-section equality is required per batch.

## Required daily learning surface

Every Day 1–30 must directly render these functions, with review/final days adapting them without deleting the function:

1. outcome and position in the learning tree;
2. prerequisites and retrieval from earlier days;
3. first-principles explanation and decisive derivation/causal mechanism;
4. at least one complete original worked reconstruction;
5. independent practice not copied from the benchmark;
6. materially changed transfer involving an assumption, representation, evidence set, constraint, loss, dependency or failure semantics;
7. difficult/advanced variant and explicit boundary;
8. evaluator with observable rubric and misconception diagnosis;
9. immutable original attempt, correction and materially changed retry;
10. scheduled delayed retrieval with fading support;
11. minimum/target timebox, Done test and evidence artifact;
12. connection to the day’s build-test-feedback-iterate output.

## Quality gates

- A day cannot PASS because headings, IDs or files exist. A semantic reviewer must approve correctness, coherence, prerequisite order, worked reasoning, exercise independence, transfer materiality, evaluator alignment and workload feasibility.
- Each capability needs requirement → day → concept → worked example → independent task → transfer → evaluator → correction → retry → retrieval → learner-evidence routing.
- Shared teaching is allowed; blanket shared evidence is not. Each ID must identify its unique reasoning contribution and failure boundary.
- Difficulty and ambiguity increase across the month while scaffolding decreases. Reviews at least weekly must retrieve rather than introduce hidden mandatory scope.
- Curriculum readiness, application delivery and learner mastery are separate statuses.

## Final-output acceptance

Curriculum readiness requires all 30 daily semantic gates, all 118 capability routes, payload equality, anti-memorization review, workload review and application-source verification. Product delivery additionally requires browser behavior verification. Learner mastery additionally requires immutable attempts, changed retries, delayed retrieval and a sealed parallel final meeting the frozen total/domain thresholds plus the full capability ledger.

## Prohibited shortcuts

No detached remediation appendix beneath unchanged lessons; no ID-range proof; no renamed benchmark questions; no number/noun-only transfer; no generated generic capsules credited as semantic review; no optional stretch used to satisfy mandatory capability; no static PASS represented as browser or learner PASS.
